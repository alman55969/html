AURTHOHIN

AURTHOHIN is a Bangladeshi rock band formed by Saidus Sumon, Tonmoy Rahman, Ponir, and Rumi Rahman. The group debuted in October 2000.
They are considered to be one of the pioneering bands in Bangladeshi alternative rock music. The band was seen as revolutionary within the rock scene in Bangladesh and played a huge part promoting rock/metal music in the country by supporting underground rock/metal bands in Bangladesh

The members are:

  • Bassbaba Sumon: Vocal, Bass
  • Shishir Ahmed: Guitar, Keyboard
  • Adnan Atique: Keyboard, Guitar
